How to link your device:
  1. Open WhatsApp on your phone.
  2. Go to Settings (iOS) or ⋮ Menu (Android) > Linked Devices.
  3. Tap "Link with phone number instead" at the bottom.
  4. Enter your phone number and type the 8-digit code above.
  5. Wait ~10 seconds. Your bot credentials will be saved automatically!
How to scan:
  1. Open WhatsApp on your phone.
  2. Go to Settings (iOS) or ⋮ Menu (Android) > Linked Devices.
  3. Tap "Link a Device".
  4. Point your phone camera at the QR code above to scan it.
